## R10 — Cost sharing and coalition stability research

- Authors: Chi-Kin Chau and Khaled Elbassioni.
- Title: Quantifying Inefficiency of Fair Cost-Sharing Mechanisms for Sharing Economy.
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05270
- Version: first submitted 2015, revised 2017; journal reference listed on the record.
- Supports: coalition formation and cost-sharing rules have trade-offs between stability and efficiency.
- Use: compare allocation rules and measure per-buyer outcomes, instead of treating equal splitting as universally fair.
- Limit: theoretical results depend on assumptions. We make no claim that our proposed mechanism inherits a theorem, is strategy-proof, or is coalition-stable.

## R11 — Group purchasing mechanism research

- Authors: Babak Hezarkhani and Greys Sošić.
- Title: Who's Afraid of Strategic Behavior? Mechanisms for Group Purchasing.
- URL: https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/17214/1/FullText.pdf
- Supports: purchase rules and cost allocation should be designed together, with attention to incentives and budget balance.

- Limit: a conceptual research reference, not proof of performance for our intended rural deployment.
