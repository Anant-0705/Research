## Proposed focus of SaathBuy

A tool for a local coordinator to assemble compatible buyer requests and explain whether a group purchase actually helps each member after all costs. The intended distinguishing workflow is visible per-person arithmetic, consent tied to a quote version, and replanning after a withdrawal. These are proposed design choices, not claims of exclusive invention or unavailability elsewhere.

SaathBuy would initially work with manually verified supplier quotes. It need not own inventory or promise instant delivery. A local shop could supply products, host collection, or coordinate orders under disclosed terms. The idea must be tested with local merchants rather than assuming they will accept it.

## Questions before claiming differentiation

Audit current provider features and local coverage. Test whether people already solve this efficiently through messaging groups. Measure the coordinator's workload. Verify that the combined offer beats the best comparable individual alternative after pickup costs. Interview people who would lose from or refuse the arrangement.
